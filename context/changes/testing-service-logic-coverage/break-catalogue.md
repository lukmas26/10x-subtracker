## Findings worth keeping

- **Rows 6–7.** Only the starter cases went red. Own-category reuse (`"VIDEO"`, `" video "`, `"łódź"`) stayed green, because after the `on conflict (user_id, lower(name)) do nothing` insert, the second lookup (`create_subscription_rpc.sql:53`, `update_subscription.sql:81`) also matches on `lower()`. Own reuse is therefore held by the unique index plus that second lookup, and the starter-first rule only by the first lookup. Each RPC's suite went red only for its own copy, so drift between the two copies is caught.
- **Rows 1 and 3.** A single wrong mapping in the service fails several cases at once (unknown id, another user's id, network), and the route test fails with it. The re-reads made it visible that nothing was written, so a wrong success could not hide behind an unchanged row.
- **Row 2.** Only the "database failure" route case catches it: the valid-add case redirects to `?saved=1` either way.
- **Not broken separately**: the Polish-letter, inner-double-space and `"S%"` cases, and the network-failure path on update. They share the code paths of rows 1, 3 and 6–7, but no single break targeted them.
